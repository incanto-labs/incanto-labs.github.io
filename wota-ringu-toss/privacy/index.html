Wota Ringu Toss — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Wota Ringu Toss (the “game”) uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Incanto Labs (“we”, “us”).

Contact (data controller): Incanto Labs, village Talwadi, P.O. Talwadi, Tharali 246482, India — incantolabs@gmail.com

The short version

  • The game is for all ages, including young children. It has no accounts, no chat, and nothing players type is ever sent anywhere.
  • On the first launch the game asks the player’s age on a neutral screen. The number is not stored or sent: the game keeps only whether the player is under 13, 13 to 15 (13 to 17 in Brazil), or older, and uses that to make ads and features safer for younger players.
  • The game shows ads (through Google AdMob) and uses Google’s Firebase tools to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. Ads shown to children are never personalised and are rated for general audiences.
  • One optional purchase, Remove Ads, goes through Google Play. It sits behind a grown-ups check.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your phone.

1. Information stored on your phone

The game keeps its own data on the phone and does not send it to us:

  • game progress, settings and the look of your toy (including any name you type on the toy’s nameplate, which never leaves the phone);
  • the age band from the age screen (child under 13 / 13–15, or 13–17 in Brazil / older) — never the age itself;
  • on phones set to Brazil or the US, the age band Google Play shares (see §5);
  • whether Remove Ads was bought, with Google Play’s receipt for it;
  • small counters that space out ads.

Uninstalling the game deletes this data. We cannot see it.

2. Information sent to Google, and why

The game uses Google services only. Each one receives what it needs:

Service What it receives Why
Google AdMob (ads), with Google’s User Messaging Platform (consent) device identifiers (the advertising ID or app set ID, as Google’s SDK decides for the player’s age), approximate location from the IP address, what ads were shown and tapped, diagnostics. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, for players 16 and over, the consent choices you make on Google’s form showing ads, measuring them, preventing fraud, recording consent
Firebase Analytics (players 13 and over only) how the game is used: buttons and screens tapped (by name, never anything typed), runs played and their results, in-game rewards earned and spent, ads watched, purchases attempted (never order numbers); plus an app instance ID, device model, operating system, language and country from the IP address understanding and improving the game
Firebase Crashlytics crash reports: stack traces, device model and operating system, a crash-reporting installation ID fixing crashes
Google Play Games Services — players 16 and over only, and only after they press Sign in the player’s Google Play Games profile (name, avatar) and their best Time-Attack score the weekly leaderboard
Google Play Billing the Remove Ads purchase, handled entirely by Google Play the purchase
Google Play in-app updates device and app version information offering a newer version of the game

Google’s own privacy policy applies to these services: https://policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone else.

3. Children

The game is designed to be safe for children of every age, and complies with Google Play’s Families policy.

  • A neutral age screen asks the player’s age on the first launch. It starts on a child’s age, cannot be skipped, and does not mention ads. Only the age band is kept.
  • For players under 13, every ad request is tagged as child-directed: Google serves only non-personalised ads, with no remarketing, rated for general audiences, through Google’s Families-certified ads SDK. Ads can be closed within 5 seconds.
  • For players 13 to 15, and 16 and 17 on phones set to Brazil, ads are tagged as under the age of consent: never personalised, in every country, and Google’s consent form is never shown to them.
  • Children are never offered Play Games sign-in, never see the leaderboard, and there is no chat or sharing anywhere in the game.
  • Purchases and the link to this policy are behind a grown-ups check that young children cannot pass.
  • Firebase Analytics never runs for players under 13: no usage data and no app instance ID is collected from them.
  • Firebase Crashlytics is used for children too, only to find and fix crashes. It receives the crash information in §2, never the advertising ID or the Android ID, and is never used for advertising.

Parents and guardians: you can ask us what information relates to your child’s device and ask us to delete it by emailing incantolabs@gmail.com. Because the game has no accounts, include the approximate date the game was installed and anything that helps us find the data; we will also explain how to remove data held by Google.

4. Europe (EEA, UK, Switzerland) — consent

Players 16 and over are shown Google’s consent form before any ad is requested. It asks about personalised ads and about analytics. Analytics collects nothing in these regions without a yes. You can change your choices at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy choices. Players under 16 are never asked; their ads are never personalised and Analytics does not run for them in these regions (nor, for players under 13, anywhere).

Legal bases (GDPR): consent for personalised ads and analytics in these regions; legitimate interests for crash reporting, fraud prevention and keeping the game working; contract for the Remove Ads purchase.

5. Brazil and the United States — age from Google Play

On phones set to Brazil or the US, the game asks Google Play for the player’s age range (Google Play Age Signals), as those laws require. Google may show its own prompt asking whether to share it. The game uses the answer only to make things safer: if Google Play says the player is younger than the age screen said, the game treats them as the younger age. Only the age band is kept on the phone; nothing is sent back.

6. How long information is kept

Information on your phone stays until you uninstall the game. Information held by Google is kept under Google’s retention policies and our settings for them: Firebase Analytics keeps event data for the period set in our Analytics settings, at most 14 months; Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. A leaderboard score belongs to the week it was earned.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Brazil’s LGPD, or California’s CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use them, email incantolabs@gmail.com. Because the game has no accounts, most information can also be removed by uninstalling the game, resetting your device’s advertising ID, or managing your Google account at https://myaccount.google.com.

8. Security

Data on the phone is stored by the game in the app’s private storage, and game saves are encrypted. Information sent to Google is sent over encrypted connections.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date. If a change needs your consent by law, we will ask for it in the game.
